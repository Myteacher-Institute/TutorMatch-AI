5. Tutor Earnings and Payment
Revenue Sharing
Tutors earn 80% of the applicable training fees paid by learners for successfully completed training delivered through the Myteacherconnect platform.
The remaining 20% is retained by Myteacherconnect to cover platform development, technology infrastructure, marketing, payment processing, customer support, administration, and operational costs.
Monthly Payments
Tutor earnings are processed and paid once every calendar month using the payment details provided by the Tutor.
Tutors are responsible for ensuring that their payment information remains accurate and up to date.
Payment Approval
To protect both learners and tutors, payments are subject to the following process:
Tutors are paid monthly for completed training where the learner has submitted a Satisfactory review or selected the Satisfactory button on the platform.
If a learner does not provide any feedback within seven (7) days after the course or training has ended, and no complaint or dispute has been submitted, the training shall automatically be deemed satisfactorily completed, and the Tutor shall become eligible for payment.
Where a learner submits an Unsatisfactory review or raises a complaint within the seven-day review period, Myteacherconnect may temporarily withhold payment while investigating the matter.
During an investigation, both the learner and the Tutor may be asked to provide relevant information or evidence.
Following its review, Myteacherconnect will determine whether payment should be released, adjusted, or withheld. The platform's decision shall be final.
